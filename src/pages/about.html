Founder’s Message

“I Don’t Just Help Businesses Grow. I Grow With Them.”

HK did not begin in a boardroom. It began over coffee in Kuwait — friends asking for a second opinion on a plan, a partnership or a new idea, while I was working as a Commercial Director. Those conversations became more frequent, and the encouragement of the people around me turned an interest into a professional practice.

From there, the work travelled. We helped brands look beyond their original markets, and in 2021 we established our headquarters in Dubai — a consultancy where franchising, brand strategy, operations and compliance sit under one roof.

What I have learned along the way is that a good plan is never enough on its own. Business success needs strategy, it needs trust, it needs disciplined execution — and it needs empathy for the people behind every business.

That is why our name matters to us. We will be honest with you, even when the answer is not the one you expected. And we will be keen — invested in your outcome as if it were our own. When you grow, we grow.

Habib Koya MarapattaFounder, HK Business Consultancy
