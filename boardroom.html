Item 02Origin

Built on experience. Driven by opportunity.

HK Business Consultancy began its journey in Kuwait. That foundation grew into a Dubai-based consultancy serving businesses, entrepreneurs and investors internationally.

From our headquarters in one of the world's most connected business hubs, we help clients think clearly about where they are going and how to get there, whether that means establishing a company, entering a new market, restructuring, or evaluating an opportunity across borders.

Our perspective is shaped by the region we come from and the markets our clients look toward: commercially grounded, personally delivered and globally minded.
