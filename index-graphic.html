01 About us

From coffee conversations
to a trusted practice.

HK Business Consultancy was founded by Habib Koya Marapatta. What began as informal advice between friends has grown into a Dubai-headquartered firm that brings franchising, brand strategy, operations and compliance together under one roof.

  1. 2015

    The spark

    Friends who took Habib’s business advice saw results — and told him: “Turn your hobby into a profession.”

  2. 2017

    The practice

    Advice became a consultancy, helping brands take their businesses into new markets and across borders.

  3. 2021

    Dubai HQ

    HK established its global headquarters in Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

  4. Today

    Growing together

    From launching start-ups to expanding legacy brands — a partner at every stage of growth.

02 Founder’s message

“I don’t just help businesses grow. I grow with them.”

What began as simple coffee conversations quickly evolved into something much bigger. I was fortunate to have friends who believed in my ideas more than I did — their encouragement is what sparked HK Business Consultancy.
