From a small town in Karnataka
to global boardrooms.

His mother ran the household like a founder runs a company — keeping cattle, cooking in large batches to support their family and local factory workers, turning a home into a hub of quiet entrepreneurial energy.

From the age of seven, Habib helped her — sticking posters, watching her manage work with grace, absorbing the grit and resourcefulness of someone building something out of very little. His love of food, people, and hustle was born in those smoke-filled kitchens, long before he had a name for any of it.

In the early 1990s, his curiosity moved beyond home. Fascinated by his brother's engineering work on the Konkan Railway tunnel through the Western Ghats, he joined Universal Engineering Company as a trainee — the first step in a career that would eventually cross 43 countries.

02 — Journey

30+ years.
One continuous journey.

  1. Early 1990s

    Universal Engineering Company

    Joined as a trainee, drawn in by his brother's engineering work on the Konkan Railway tunnel through the Western Ghats — years of on-ground learning that would later make him the company's Executive Director.

  2. 1995

    Domino's Pizza, Saudi Arabia

    Started as a delivery boy, rose to Deputy District Manager and then Senior Operations Manager. Helped bring the Malaysian brand Marrybrown to India along the way.

    • Fastest Pizza Maker, Domino's Pizza KSA (1998)
    • Best Manager of the Month — May 1998 & February 1999
    • 5 International Sales Achievement Awards from Domino's USA
    • Best Deputy District Manager, South India (May 2003)
  3. 2005

    Starbucks Coffee, Kuwait

    Joined as District Manager. A regular customer, impressed by his dedication, made an offer that would change the course of his career.

    • Starbucks Bravo Award for exceeding budgeted sales targets
  4. United States → Middle East

    Auntie Anne's

    Trained in the U.S. on the brand's own operating model, then built its Middle East presence from the ground up — including expansion into Kuwait's Avenue Mall and the brand's first outlet in Basra, Iraq.

    “If you join me, I'll bring a brand from America for you to build.”

    Seven successful stores opened across Kuwait and Iraq.

  5. 2009

    Costa Coffee → Hussein Morad Bebehani Group

    Joined Costa Coffee as General Manager, then became Commercial Director of the entire Hussein Morad Bebehani Group — bringing Costa Coffee, Van Cleef & Arpels and Bvlgari into Kuwait, the Van Cleef & Arpels rights secured through a personal pitch to the brand's President in Paris.

    • International Leadership Award, Costa Coffee (2010)
    • 55% consistent sales increase, Costa Coffee Kuwait (2010)
    • Hosted the Kuwait Coffee Ambassador Event
    • Opened 17 Costa Coffee stores across Kuwait
  6. 2016

    Relax Business Solutions

    Years of informal mentoring — coffee, conversation, advice for founders — became his first venture of his own.

  7. 2021

    HK Consultancy

    A boutique business and brand consultancy, built to turn three decades of global operating experience into structured support for retail, F&B, and lifestyle brands across the Middle East and India.

The business was never just the business.

Koya's favorite pastime was sipping coffee with close friends and helping them grow their businesses. Many of those casual sessions quietly turned into multi-million-dollar successes — the advice itself had nowhere formal to live, until it did.

Over thirty years and 43 countries, the constant was never the category — food, coffee, or luxury retail — it was people: teams built from 18+ nationalities, and a reputation built on human-centered leadership rather than brilliance alone.
