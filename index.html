About us

From coffee conversations to a global practice.

HK Business Consultancy was founded by Habib Koya Marapatta. What began as informal advice between friends has grown into a Dubai-headquartered firm that brings franchising, brand strategy, operations and compliance together under one roof.

  1. 2015

    As a Commercial Director in Kuwait, Habib began offering business advice to friends. Their success led them to say: “Turn your hobby into a profession.”

  2. 2017

    The advice became a practice — helping brands take their businesses into new markets and across borders.

  3. 2021

    HK established its global headquarters in Dubai.

  4. Today

    From launching start-ups to expanding legacy brands, HK is a trusted partner for businesses at every stage of growth.

Founder’s message

“I don’t just help businesses grow. I grow with them.”

What began as simple coffee conversations in Kuwait quickly evolved into something much bigger. I was fortunate to have friends who believed in my ideas more than I did — their encouragement is what sparked HK Business Consultancy.

Over the years, I’ve learned that success is never built in isolation. It takes strategy, trust, execution and empathy. Our mission is to walk with you through every challenge, every pivot and every breakthrough.

We don’t just give you a plan. We build your future with you.

Habib Koya Marapatta Founder
