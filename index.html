Our perspective

One perspective.
Multiple disciplines.

HK does not sell separate services. It connects legal, brand, franchise, technology, investment and trading disciplines around a single client objective, so nothing has to be stitched together across agencies.

Whether a client is establishing a company, entering a new market, restructuring, or evaluating an opportunity across borders, the work starts with the commercial objective and builds the structure, brand and operations around it.

Strategic thinking

We begin by understanding the business, its objectives and its market before recommending a route.

ii

iii

Practical execution

We help turn strategy into action: formation, documentation, launch and growth, coordinated in one place.

About HK

From coffee conversations to a Dubai-headquartered group.

HK Business Consultancy began in Kuwait, where informal business advice among friends grew into a consultancy. In 2021 it established its global headquarters in Dubai, bringing legal strategy, branding, franchising and operational support under one roof.

Today HK sits at the centre of a small group of companies, including Maplitho (growth and digital marketing) and RaSynth (technology), with a presence across the UAE, India, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman and Malaysia. Its mission is simple: we grow when you grow.

  1. 2015

    Kuwait

    Coffee-table beginnings

    Habib Koya Marapatta, then a Commercial Director, offers business advice to friends. Their success leads to one line: “Turn your hobby into a profession.”

  2. 2017

    Cross-border

    Brands begin to move

    Habib starts helping brands expand across borders, taking the informal consultancy toward structured, cross-market work.

  3. 2021

    Dubai

    A global headquarters

    HK's global headquarters is established in Dubai, combining legal strategy, branding, franchising and operational support.

  4. Today

    GCC · India · Beyond

    A group, not a single firm

    A presence spanning the UAE, India, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman and Malaysia, supported by a group of companies.

Chairman's message

Habib Koya Marapatta

Chairman & Founder

What began as simple coffee conversations in Kuwait quickly evolved into something much bigger. I was fortunate to have friends who believed in my ideas more than I did. Their encouragement is what sparked HK Consultancy.

Over the years, I’ve learned that success is never built in isolation. It takes strategy, trust, execution, and empathy. At HK Consultancy, our mission is to walk with you through every challenge, every pivot, and every breakthrough.

“I don’t just help businesses grow. I grow with them.”

Operating background

Before founding HK, Habib built an operating career in food & beverage and luxury retail across the Gulf: from Domino’s Pizza in Saudi Arabia, to District Manager at Starbucks Kuwait, building Auntie Anne’s Middle East presence, and General Manager of Costa Coffee in Kuwait. As Commercial Director of the Hussein Morad Bebehani Group, he was involved in bringing Costa Coffee, Van Cleef & Arpels and Bvlgari into Kuwait.
